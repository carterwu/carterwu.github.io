# Overview vs. Trajectory in Engineering Evaluation

When evaluating engineering performance, we can take two perspectives:

1. **Overview (Snapshot)** — Assessing the current state of the codebase, commit history, and PRs to gauge quality and discipline.
2. **Trajectory (Sequence)** — Analyzing the sequence of commits, PRs, and reviews over time to understand the engineer's development, responsiveness, and iteration patterns. And run tests to verify actual correctness, build stability, and catch runtime issues on different checkpoints to view the engineer's consistency and reliability.

The overview approach is like looking at a single frame of a movie — it can tell you a lot about the current quality and style, but it misses the story of how we got there. The trajectory approach is like watching the whole movie — it reveals patterns of growth, responsiveness to feedback, and how the engineer iterates on their work.
